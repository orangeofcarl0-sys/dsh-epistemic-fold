The scheduler choice favored a short-lived process model over durability. A durable outbox was considered but rejected because its required storage backing is unavailable in this deployment; adopting it would have introduced an unusable dependency. Simplicity and the limited process lifetime made the lighter queue design the better fit. Next step: wire the scheduler to the selected queue abstraction and verify that integration does not introduce a storage-backed initialization path or other dependency on the rejected outbox.
