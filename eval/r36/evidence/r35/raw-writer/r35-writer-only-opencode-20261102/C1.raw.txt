The key risk was relying on an event source that cannot guarantee delivery; retry/debounce around it would not help because missing notifications are unrecoverable. That ruled out keeping the native path as the primary mechanism. Polling trades latency and I/O for determinism, so the next step is to validate interval behavior and scan scope under realistic churn, including rapid create/delete/rename sequences, before integration with the cache client. The open engineering question is whether the polling interval is tight enough for the cache's freshness needs without excessive filesystem load; that should be measured rather than guessed.
