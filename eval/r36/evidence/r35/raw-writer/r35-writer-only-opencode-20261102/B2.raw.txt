The scheduler design favored operational simplicity over durability: an in-memory queue avoids adding infrastructure for a short-lived process. A durable outbox was considered but ruled out on deployment-dependency grounds, so retaining the in-memory approach follows from what this environment can support rather than from a preference for losing queued work. The next step is to confirm the scheduler stays dependency-free and that any persistence assumptions are removed or explicitly guarded.
