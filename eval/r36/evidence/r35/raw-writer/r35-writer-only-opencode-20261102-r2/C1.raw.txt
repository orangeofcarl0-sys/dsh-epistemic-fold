Completeness was prioritized over watch latency because a cache client cannot tolerate stale entries from dropped filesystem notifications. The native watch path was rejected for the shipping platform because its delivery guarantees are not strong enough; retries or platform-specific workarounds would preserve the same failure mode. Polling was chosen as the predictable fallback, accepting higher scan cost and detection delay in exchange for bounded, repeatable behavior. The next step is to wire the polling backend into the cache-client integration and verify the polling interval and directory scope are explicit, configurable, and covered at the same platform boundary.
</handoff>
